# Weekly Status Report Instructions

Use this template to complete a clean weekly report for the delivery team and SteerCo.

## How to fill out each section

### 1. Executive Summary
Summarize the week in 2-3 short paragraphs. Include key progress, any major decisions, and whether the sprint is on track.

### 2. Delivery Progress
- Sprint/Project: enter the sprint name or project name.
- Current status: summarize overall status, such as on track, at risk, or delayed.
- Completed this week: list the most important deliverables completed.
- In progress: list major workstreams still in progress.
- Planned next week: describe the top priorities for next week.

### 3. Risks and Issues
- Top risk: describe the highest-impact risk.
- Status/impact: explain current status and impact on timeline, budget, or quality.
- Mitigation actions: describe what is being done to reduce the risk.

### 4. Budget and Forecast
- Planned spend: the expected T&M budget for the reporting period.
- Actual spend: the actual spend year-to-date or for the sprint.
- Burn rate: current rate of budget consumption.
- Remaining budget: how much budget is left.
- Forecasted variance: expected over/under spend.

### 5. Resource Utilization
- Total team capacity: the total available effort across the team.
- Utilization by role: show capacity usage for key roles.
- Capacity concerns: note any roles that are over-/under-utilized or at risk.

### 6. Quality / QA Status
- Open defects by severity: summarize critical, high, and medium defects.
- Test execution summary: note pass/fail rates or test progress.
- Quality incidents: describe any incidents affecting quality.
- Release risk: highlight risks to upcoming release readiness.

### 7. Blocked Work Summary
- Total blocked issues: count of issues currently blocked.
- Key blocked items: the most critical blocked issues.
- Next steps to unblock: actions to resolve blockers.

### 8. Prior Sprint Comparison
- Sprint 1 status summary: previous sprint summary.
- Sprint 2 status summary: earlier sprint summary.
- Trend observations: compare progress, scope, or risk trends.

### 9. Action Items / Decisions
- Item 1: list a key action or decision taken this week.
- Item 2: list another action or decision.
- Item 3: list any follow-up actions for next week.

## Notes
- Keep language concise and focused on stakeholder priorities.
- Use bullet points for clarity.
- Keep the report professional and suitable for SteerCo review.
